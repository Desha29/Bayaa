# Reusable presentation units

ErpSidebar: layout; destination and selection callbacks.
ErpDesktopHeader: layout; title, icon, and actions.
ErpMetricCard: basic; label/value/icon.
ErpRecordsView: basic; typed display records and specification.
ErpFormDialog: basic; localized field specifications and preview-only save.
Shimmer and SkeletonList: basic; reduced-motion-aware loading presentation.
